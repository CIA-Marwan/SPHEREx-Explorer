# Demo preparation

The [2026 guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) requires a public link to **either** a video of at most 30 seconds **or** an English slide deck of at most seven slides including title/intro slides. No demo has been recorded, published or verified. Do not include recognizable names, voices or likenesses of minors; obtain required consent for adults. Verify artwork permissions and SI watermarks before publication.

## Suggested 30-second video script

0–4s: Show Home. “SPHEREx Explorer brings real infrared archive observations to the public.”

4–10s: Open Explore and choose the saved QR3 observation. “Inspect real NASA/IPAC cutouts, dates, detector bands and sources.”

10–20s: Open Compare, move the divider and select side-by-side. “A screened DR1 pair is reprojected to a common grid for visual comparison.”

20–25s: Show the metadata and limitations. “Apparent differences aren't confirmed discoveries; this is not a scientific difference image.”

25–30s: Show About and SIMBAD resolver. “Sources, AI assistance and limitations are disclosed. Arbitrary-coordinate sequences are not yet available.”

Record a concise screen capture, verify duration and readable SI labels, then upload it publicly without login and test the link in a private browser. Alternatively make an English deck of up to seven slides using the same storyline. Do not invent a URL.
