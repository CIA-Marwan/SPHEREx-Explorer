# Submission checklist

The field names below come from the public [2026 Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/); the authenticated form itself was not inspected and may contain additional requirements.

## Before submitting

- [ ] Ask the Local Lead about project work predating the event and disclose the timeline.
- [ ] Confirm team registration, challenge, roster, event, local time zone and event-specific deadlines/rules.
- [ ] Verify rights, all third-party licenses, SI disclosure, favicon provenance, and image/video watermarks.
- [x] Publish and test a public no-login final-project link: https://cia-marwan.github.io/SPHEREx-Explorer/ (static version).
- [ ] Publish and test a public no-login demo link.

## Manual handoff

1. Sign in to NASA Space Apps; select **My Team**, then its **Project** tab. Verify the selected challenge and event.
2. Fill every required field, add verified public links, and use **Save Progress** frequently. Check the actual form for additional fields.
3. Before the official deadline in the relevant event time zone, an authorized team member must choose **Submit for Judging**. Do not submit without team approval.
4. Verify the submitted banner/confirmation, reopen the project and test links without authentication. Save confirmation evidence. No submission has yet been made.
